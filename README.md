# Crime Analytics Dashboard using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Crime Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze crime incidents across different crime types, years, countries, resolution status, and people involved.

The dashboard converts crime data into meaningful visual insights using KPI cards, charts, and geographical visualization.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Analyze the overall number of crime incidents.
- Compare resolved and unresolved crime cases.
- Calculate the overall crime resolution rate.
- Identify the most frequently occurring crime types.
- Analyze crime incidents across different years.
- Understand the number of people involved in different crime categories.
- Analyze the geographical distribution of crime incidents across countries.
- Present crime-related insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Crime data contains information about crime types, incidents, countries, years, resolution status, and people involved. Manually analyzing these records can make it difficult to identify major crime patterns, yearly changes, geographical distribution, and case resolution status.

Therefore, an interactive **Crime Analytics Dashboard** was developed using Power BI to transform raw crime data into meaningful and easy-to-understand insights.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

1. What is the total number of crime cases recorded?
2. How many crime cases are resolved and unresolved?
3. What is the overall crime resolution rate?
4. What percentage of cases are resolved?
5. Which crime type occurs most frequently?
6. How have crime incidents changed over the years?
7. Which countries are represented in the dataset?
8. How many people are involved in different crime types?
9. Which crime type has the highest number of people involved?
10. What are the major patterns observed across crime types, years, countries, and resolution status?

## 🛠️ Data Preparation

The crime dataset was prepared using **Power Query in Power BI**.

- Checked and prepared appropriate data types.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Crime Resolution Analysis

A donut chart is used to compare **resolved and unresolved crime cases**.

It provides a quick overview of the distribution of cases based on their resolution status.

### 2. Crime Type Analysis

A bar chart compares the number of incidents across different crime categories.

**Violence and Sexual Offences** is the highest displayed crime category with **959 cases**.

Other displayed categories include:

- Vehicle Crime
- Shoplifting
- Theft from a Person
- Robbery and Mugging

### 3. Crime Incident Change Over Year

A line/area chart shows the change in crime incidents across 2021, 2022, and 2023.

| Year | Crime Incidents |
|---|---:|
| 2021 | 405 |
| 2022 | 1,150 |
| 2023 | 945 |

The visualization helps identify changes in crime incidents over time.

### 4. Crime Distribution by Country

A map visualization displays crime incidents geographically.

The dashboard covers **13 countries**, allowing users to explore the geographical distribution of crime cases.

### 5. People Involved by Crime

A column chart compares the number of people involved across different crime categories.

**Violence and Sexual Offences** has the highest displayed number of people involved among the shown categories.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- The dashboard contains approximately **3K crime cases**.
- Around **2K cases are resolved**, while **749 cases remain unresolved**.
- The overall crime **resolution rate is 70.04%**.
- The dataset covers **13 countries**.
- Approximately **7K people** are involved across the displayed crime categories.
- **Violence and Sexual Offences** is the highest displayed crime category with **959 cases**.
- Crime incidents increased from **405 in 2021 to 1,150 in 2022**.
- Crime incidents then decreased from **1,150 in 2022 to 945 in 2023**.
- The people-involved analysis shows a higher involvement count for **Violence and Sexual Offences** compared with the other displayed categories.
- The country map provides a geographical view of crime incidents.
- The dashboard combines crime type, yearly trends, resolution status, countries, and people involved into one analytical report.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of crime incidents and their resolution status. It helps users understand frequently occurring crime categories, yearly changes, geographical distribution, and people involved across different crime types.

The analysis can support **crime pattern monitoring, reporting, and data-driven understanding of crime trends**.
